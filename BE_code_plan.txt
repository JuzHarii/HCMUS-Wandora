Sprint 2 - Correctness and Data Integrity Hardening
- Fix critical gaps from previous review:

  3) Actually honor force_regenerate in itinerary generation logic.
  4) Harden time parsing with safe fallback validation.

- Add idempotency checks around regeneration/adjust flows.
